TAT Buckets & Formulas

Samsung SALT Standard
Visit TAT: 0~10 vs >10

0~10: TotalVisitTime ≤ 10 min. Evaluated as potentially rushed call. Percentage is Count(0~10) / HC.

Service Time TAT: 0~20 vs >20

0~20: TotalServiceTime ≤ 20 min. Percentage is Count(0~20) / HC.

B2B Calls: Commercial & LFD

Identified via B2B flag, Monitor models (LH/QM/QB/QH), Commercial displays, or IIDM. Tracked separately in B2B Visit Time & Service Time.
